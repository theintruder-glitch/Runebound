# Four Kingdoms art and audio

The existing Thornwatch exterior was retained. Three new exteriors were generated with the built-in image-generation tool: frostspire.png, blackmire.png and stormbreak.png (each 1254 square RGBA). A new realm_environment.png (1536 x 1024 RGBA) contains six isolated atlas cells: rocky mountain, snowy mountain, oak, swamp tree, mossy boulders and snow pine. Original images remain intact; AtlasTexture selects cells at runtime. No generated image was edited with a local image-processing script.

Prompt direction: isolated whole gothic structures and compact elevated foundations; weathered gray/black stone, gold finials, readable bottom-center gates, matching elevated viewing angles; Frostspire black spires/blue windows/snow/glaciers, Blackmire broad sunken towers/chains/roots/purple flooded gate, Stormbreak separated towers and arched skybridges over a cliff foundation. Thornwatch was an artistic reference, not a template to recolor. Environment prompt requested a 3-column, 2-row transparent atlas, realistic textured materials, compact silhouettes and exposed roots. Images belong to the current project under assets/.

The website's live view was inaccessible behind a sign-in wall in this environment. Do not claim these are downloaded website artworks or that exact current-site matching was verified.

The original enemy/prop atlases, music, licenses and credits remain. Regional ambience is original deterministic synthesis, generated by tools/build_realm_audio.py; no sampled recordings or third-party songs. Runtime uses the existing four-voice pool and one music player.

Texture budgets: about 6 MiB base RGBA for the environment atlas and each visible castle before engine overhead. Thornwatch is preloaded; additional castle textures are loaded on nearby draw and released when the active outdoor region changes. Only nearby terrain/scenery and one interior floor are active. Mobile hardware memory/GPU measurements remain pending.
