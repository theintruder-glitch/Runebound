AUTO PICKUP UPDATE — 0.11.1
Install Runebound-Godot-v0.11.1.apk over your existing Runebound Godot app. Keep the app installed to retain your character.
Walk near wild resources to pick them up. Pause > Auto pickup turns this on or off. Enemy rewards are already automatic. Chests and gardens keep their normal buttons.

PLAY:
Install Runebound-Godot-v0.12.0.apk and open Runebound Godot.
No editor or Termux is needed to play.

EDIT:
Extract this ZIP, open Godot's Android editor, Import project.godot,
then open the project and press Play.

TRANSFER YOUR OLD CHARACTER:
1. Install migration/Runebound-v0.2.1-Save-Transfer.apk over old Runebound.
2. Continue old character > Pause > Export character for Godot.
3. Save the JSON file to Downloads.
4. Open Runebound Godot > Import a character save > select the JSON.

Do not uninstall the old app to apply the Save Transfer update.
Read README.md for source details and Android export instructions.

UPDATE: Install over Runebound Godot without uninstalling.
Hold the joystick to walk continuously; release it to stop.
Character drawing and walking animation now update every frame.
The camera scrolls continuously at the view boundary, without area jumps.
Pause shows Version 0.12.0 / Laptop Edition.
Pause > Low power selects 30 FPS (on) or 60 FPS (off).

CAMP AND CRAFTING:
1. Walk to labeled resources and tap GATHER.
2. Tap Camp at the top of the screen.
3. Make Planks and Rope, then build a Shelter.
4. Build a Campfire using Wood and Stone.
5. Stay near the fire to cook berries or herbal stew.
6. Eat cooked food from Camp; add Wood to refuel the fire.
7. Build a Workbench to craft bows and swords.
Walk inside your shelter's green circle to rest safely.

ROTATION AND CONTROLS:
Turn the phone sideways for landscape.
Or open Pause > Landscape view.
Pause > Auto-rotate view restores automatic portrait/landscape rotation.
The joystick, attack, spell, and dash buttons are larger.

HEART OF MOSSVEIL:
Follow the objective below the health bars: gather wood, build a fire,
cook berries, then build a shelter. The shrine is northeast of the start.
Avoid red circles when fighting the Root Warden.
Win to unlock Arcane Archer; claim your unique weapon at the shrine.

BUILDING:
Camp > Place a structure > move to position its ghost > ROTATE > PLACE.
CANCEL spends nothing. Open Camp nearby to move or dismantle a structure.
Beds set your return point; storage chests hold gathered materials.
Pause > Sound and music lets you mute the original music and effects.

0.7.0 THUMB LAYOUT:
Landscape joystick sits farther inward from the left edge.
Portrait spells are along the bottom, with combat controls above them.

FOUR REALMS (0.7.0):
Follow the opening guide through Mossveil Ruins and your first dungeon floor.
Camp > Explore the regions lets you track regional landmarks.
Caves are east, swamp west, and snow north.
Discover landmarks to learn unique recipes and attempt hidden-class trials.
Your class skill button is above the joystick; Q works on a keyboard.
Wolves hunt in packs. Archers keep distance. Deer flee and can be hunted.

WAYFINDER (0.7.0):
Gear: tap an item card to inspect, equip, upgrade, enchant, or salvage.
Camp > Build places structures.
Camp > Craft > Parts makes planks and rope.
Camp > Craft > Food cooks meals; Supplies lets you eat carried food.
Camp > Journal lists discoveries and challenges.
World map is in Camp and Pause (M on keyboard).
Tap a marker, then Track selected. Home and ruins can be tracked too.

TURNING SEASONS (0.8.0):
The world now cycles through Spring, Summer, Autumn and Winter.
Each season lasts 20 minutes of active play. Time pauses in menus and away.
Weather varies by region; the northern region keeps its snowy ground.
Camp > Journal shows your season, day and bonus gathering materials.
Stand under a Roof or inside a Shelter near a lit Campfire for bonus recovery.
Cook Warm tea at a lit fire; drink it under Camp > Supplies.
Pause > Weather effects turns particles and fog off if desired.
Install this APK over your current Runebound Godot app; do not uninstall.

FRONTIER JOBS (0.9.0):
Open Camp > Quest board and active jobs > Track quest board.
A wooden board stands northwest of your original starting camp.
Walk close to accept up to three jobs from Posted.
Review progress under Active and track one on the HUD.
Return to the board to turn in; deliveries consume carried supplies.
Every third completed job adds Rare enchanted gear; every ninth is Epic.
Keep a free Gear slot for the completion bonus.
Jobs repost after ten active-play minutes. Progress is saved.

TOUCH SCROLL PATCH (0.9.1):
Swipe up or down over the inventory cards, upgrade buttons or descriptions.
You no longer need to grab the scrollbar or find empty background.
Tap normally to select or upgrade. Swipes do not spend gold or stat points.
Install over the current Runebound Godot app without uninstalling.

CAMP TRADER (0.10.0):
Open Camp > Trader Mara > Track Mara.
Her stall is northeast of your original starting camp.
Walk close to buy, sell or recover equipment under Buyback.
Supplies, meals, Rare enchanted weapons and an Epic relic are available.
Stock refreshes each active game day; opening menus does not reroll it.
Your last eight sold equipment pieces can be bought back with a small fee.
Six completed board jobs earn 10% off; eighteen earn 15% off.
Normal swipe scrolling is retained. Install over the current app.

CAMP FARMING (0.11.0):
Open Camp > Garden plots > Place a Garden.
Each plot needs 4 Wood, 2 Stone and 2 Fiber. The limit is 12 gardens.
Walk near your plot and tap TEND GARDEN.
Plant 2 Berries for 6, or 2 Herbs for 5.
Discover the Veil Altar to plant 2 Glowcap for 5.
Crops take about 3-4 active minutes in summer or autumn.
Spring grows 25% faster; winter grows 25% slower.
Explore while crops grow. Menus and time away pause progress.
Ready crops stay ready: return and tap Harvest to collect ingredients.
Move plots without losing crops. Clear returns the planting ingredients.
Harvest or clear a crop before dismantling its plot.
Swipe normally over planting and harvesting buttons to scroll.
Install over the current Runebound Godot app without uninstalling.

LAPTOP AND CHARACTER TRANSFER (0.12.0):
The phone controls and existing character stay in place after updating.
To bring your character to Windows:
1. Pause > Export character save > choose Downloads.
2. Move Runebound-Character.json to the laptop.
3. Laptop title > Import a character save > select that JSON.
Devices keep separate local saves after transfer; there is no automatic sync.
Export the receiving device's old character first if you want to keep it.
