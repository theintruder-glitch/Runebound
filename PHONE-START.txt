RUNEBOUND 0.21.0 — ANDROID

1. In your current Runebound Godot app, use Pause > Export character save.
2. Download Runebound-Godot-v0.21.0.apk and install it over the existing app.
3. Keep the app installed; do not uninstall or clear its data.
4. Continue your character, or choose Begin a new journey if the old app data was already cleared.

MOVE: left joystick. ATTACK: weapon. DASH: dodge. Spells and class skill have their existing buttons.
Gear/Camp menus: swipe the content to scroll; tap actions normally.
Pause: orientation, battery saver, sound, weather effects and character export.

SURVIVAL: eat, drink, sleep and keep warm. Rain/snow soak clothing, making cold worse.
A roof or shelter blocks precipitation. A reachable lit fire dries clothing quickly.
Walls block warmth and bed interaction. Classic mode has no needs drain.

This update uses app.runebound.godot, version code 29 and the original prototype certificate.
If Android rejects the update, keep the current app and save. Report the exact message.
Physical installation and phone performance still need playtesting; automated engine checks are separate.

NEW GROUND SPELL AIMING:
Learn Meteor Fall (level 5) or Blizzard (level 7) from Mara, then assign it in the spellbook.
Tap its bottom spell button, tap/drag a ground spot, then tap CAST. Tap CANCEL or Back to stop.
The preview is free. Moving, enemies and Survival needs remain live while you aim.
CAST is disabled if a wall blocks the selected point. Dodge cancels an unfinished cast.
This applies to learned hotbar spells; the Mage class skill keeps its existing behavior.

BASIC ATTACKS:
Face toward enemies for melee. Attacks strike ahead, not behind you. Misses still animate.
Ordinary foes briefly recoil from clean melee hits; bosses resist this interruption.

CHAPTER I — THE HOLLOW ROOT:
Open Camp > Chapter I, then visit Mara to begin. Investigate the three camps, return to Mara,
then choose the story expedition at Mossveil Ruins. It has three floors and a named final boss.
Purple rune circles warn before they strike; move away or dodge. Return to Mara to claim Hearthcall.
Your old tutorial, shrine and regular dungeons stay available.

ENEMY TACTICS:
Dungeon archers and wisps hold their firing distance. Close in to force them back; cornered shooters
stand their ground. A gold dashed line connects a shooter to its locked warning circle.
Move out of the circle or dodge. Walls interrupt pending attacks; melee enemies spread as they approach.
Chapter I and your existing character remain available.

DREADWOOD GRAPHICS:
All graphics qualities include the new scary creature/boss designs and detailed trees/chests.
Pause > Graphics: Performance is the phone default. Try Balanced for soft dungeon/camp lighting.
Rich adds detail; return to Performance if your phone feels warm or slow.
Low power still caps the game at 30 FPS and limits soft lights to two.
Bosses look larger; their hitboxes and warning/dodge rules have not changed.

STUCK IN SNOW / MAP CORNER FIX:
Install over the current app. Continue the same character.
Pause > Recover stuck character > Return to original camp - keep my character.
You stay paused at camp: review supplies if needed, then Resume journey.
Gear, level, quests, camp and gold remain. No health/mana/survival refill is granted.
Maps now center on distant characters. Around me centers on you; World overview shows you and home.

CAMP PROVISIONS:
Camp > Craft > Food or Parts > Choose quantity on a recipe.
Choose an exact amount with plus/minus, a 1/5/10 preset, or Maximum (up to 99).
Review carried ingredients and shortages, then tap Make. Back spends nothing.
Use a reachable lit fire for cooking and a reachable bench for gear. Walls block station access.
Cooking creates food for your bag; eat it separately under Supplies.
